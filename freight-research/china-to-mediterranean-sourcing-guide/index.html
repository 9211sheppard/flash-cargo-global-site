China to the Mediterranean: Supplier Checks

Market context only. This index reading is not used to price, settle or compare your order.

Start with the buying decision

Record the intended use, product specification, quantity and unit, material, finish, packaging and destination. Keep essential requirements separate from preferences. A supplier needs to know which details are fixed and which still need a sample or a documented answer.

Ask for a comparable quote rather than the lowest unexplained total. Identify what is included, what remains unconfirmed and how long the offer is valid. Do not turn an indicative ocean index into an itemized delivered price.

The reading and its limits

Freightos FBX13 covers China/East Asia to the Mediterranean in 40ft units, across multiple gateways and jurisdictions.

A regional index does not establish the price for every port, a different container size, a shared container or final delivery. Read the publisher’s route definition to understand the regional coverage.

Only a single attributed public 40ft FBX weekly reading is reproduced here. It is general information, not an order price or a calculated average. A genuine dated written quote can be shown only with publication approval; other amounts remain quote required.

Confirm the actual handoff

  • Name the destination country and port. A regional index does not make different national import procedures or inland delivery costs interchangeable.
  • Agree product descriptions, packaging, sample references and quality-check responsibilities before the supplier prepares the order for dispatch.
  • Separate a port-to-port comparison from the buyer’s delivered requirement. Ask for the destination charges, importer responsibilities and final receiving scope in writing.

Keep quality evidence with the order

Identify the approved sample and specification revision. Confirm the checks needed before release and keep photographs, findings and unresolved issues together. Third-party inspection can be booked on request; its scope and charges must be agreed. An inspection is not a blanket product certification.

Record the invoice description, package count, dimensions, weight and receiving restrictions for the actual goods. Ask the responsible importer and qualified service providers to review product-specific obligations. No generic duty, tax or delivery promise is supplied here.

Use an itemized comparison

Separate product cost, sample and inspection costs, origin work, transport, destination work, applicable duty and tax, and final delivery. Unknown amounts remain quote required. Keep all compared amounts in one stated currency and identify exclusions before a purchase decision.
