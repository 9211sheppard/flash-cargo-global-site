Turkey furniture supplier to U.S. hotel project freight checklist

Freight planning questions for furniture, fixtures, and project cargo moving from Turkey to U.S. hospitality or commercial sites.

Furniture and fixture shipments are rarely just cartons on a dock. For hotel, retail, or commercial projects, the freight plan should include crate count, dimensions, weight, fragility, finish protection, delivery appointment, staging needs, inside delivery constraints, elevator access, floor delivery, installation timing, and whether photos are needed before and after handoff.

The supplier should separate stock replenishment from project cargo. Project cargo often has a receiver schedule, site readiness problem, and damage-visibility risk that should be reviewed before choosing ocean, air, warehouse staging, or white glove delivery planning.
