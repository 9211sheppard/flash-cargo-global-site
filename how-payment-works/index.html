How Payment Works.

Every order runs the same way: a written quote, staged payments, and the agreed checks before shipment.

A written quote comes first

Nothing is paid before a written, itemized quote. The quote identifies the product, quantity, currency, delivery scope, inclusions, exclusions and the agreed payment stages.

Supplier payment is staged

Supplier payment is staged: a deposit, then the balance only after sample approval and any agreed pre-shipment inspection. The approved sample, agreed checks and acceptance decision belong in the order record.

Pay a business, not a person

Payments go only to a business account or a recognized processor’s invoice, never a personal account. The business payee and payment instructions must match the written order agreement.

Verify any change before paying

We never change bank or payment details by email. Any change is confirmed through the request form before you pay.

No surprise fee requests

No surprise “release”, “customs” or “insurance” fees outside the written quote. Any proposed change must be itemized and agreed in writing before payment.

Agree the remedy before payment

If goods fail inspection, the refund or replacement terms are written plainly. The order agreement identifies the remedy, responsibilities, applicable costs and timing before payment; no unspecified automatic refund, escrow or insurance protection is implied.

Verify an unexpected request

We only email from @flashcargoglobal.com and never request payment by gift card, crypto or a personal account.

If payment details change or a request does not match the agreed quote, stop and use the request form on this website to verify it. Do not send payment-card details, passwords or bank credentials through the enquiry form.
