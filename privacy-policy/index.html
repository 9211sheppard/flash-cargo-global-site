Privacy

Privacy Policy

Updated 2026-10-23

Cargo Global uses the information you choose to submit to review product sourcing and delivery requests, prepare quotes and coordinate approved orders.

Information submitted with a request

A request may include product specifications, quantities, a target price and currency, reference links and a delivery location. The current intake form also asks for private contact details to allow follow-up. These details are not published as a business directory or displayed in public enquiry listings. Please do not submit payment-card details, passwords, identity documents, health information or confidential material that is not needed to review the request.

How request information is used

Information is used to assess feasibility, clarify specifications, compare supplier options, prepare a quote and, where you approve an order, coordinate samples, inspections, purchase and delivery. A request is not permission to place an order or charge a payment.

Hosting and request handling

The public website is hosted using GitHub Pages. The request handler runs on Cloudflare Workers and stores submitted enquiries in Cloudflare storage. These providers may process technical information such as an IP address, request time and browser information to operate and protect their services. Existing business mail is provided through Microsoft 365. Automatic form-to-email delivery is not currently enabled.

Sharing for an approved request or order

Product and delivery information may need to be shared with relevant suppliers, inspectors, carriers or customs service providers to assess or fulfil the request. Only information relevant to that work should be shared. Do not include third-party personal information or proprietary documents unless you are authorized to provide them.

Reference files and links

A linked photograph or specification remains subject to the privacy and access settings of the service hosting it. Avoid links that publicly expose unrelated documents. File-upload handling is not enabled in the current intake form.

Cookies and analytics

This release does not include a shared advertising pixel, a Google Analytics tag or a tag-manager container. Hosting and security providers may still process necessary technical data. External source links have their own privacy practices.

Retention and access

Request information should be kept only while it is needed for review, an approved order, applicable recordkeeping or a legitimate security purpose. A fixed retention schedule has not yet been established for the request store. You may ask to access, correct or remove information you submitted through the online contact route, subject to applicable legal requirements and our ability to verify the request.

Cross-border processing and security

Providers and approved order partners may process information outside your country. Access should be limited to people and providers who need it for the request or order. No internet service can promise absolute security. Do not send sensitive documents through the general request form.

Questions or changes

Use the online request page for privacy enquiries. This page may be updated when our services or request-handling arrangements change. The date above identifies the current version.
