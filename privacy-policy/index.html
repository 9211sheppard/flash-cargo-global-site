Privacy

Privacy Policy

Updated 2026-10-24

Cargo Global uses the information you choose to submit to review product sourcing and delivery requests, prepare quotes and coordinate approved orders.

Information submitted with a request

Buyer and delivery requests include product requirements, quantities, budget, destination, timeline and a private reply email. Supplier applications include company capability, production country, minimum orders, capacity, documents, export markets, website and sample availability. Personal names and phone numbers are not requested. Submitted information is not published in a public supplier directory. Do not submit payment-card details, passwords, identity documents or unrelated confidential material.

How request information is used

Information is used to assess feasibility, clarify specifications, compare supplier options, prepare a quote and, where you approve an order, coordinate samples, inspections, purchase and delivery. A request is not permission to place an order or charge a payment.

Hosting and request handling

The public website is hosted using GitHub Pages. The request handler runs on Cloudflare Workers and stores submitted enquiries in Cloudflare storage for review. These providers may process technical information such as an IP address, request time and browser information to operate and protect their services. Existing business mail is provided through Microsoft 365.

Sharing for an approved request or order

Product and delivery information may need to be shared with relevant suppliers, inspectors, carriers or customs service providers to assess or fulfil the request. Only information relevant to that work should be shared. Do not include third-party personal information or proprietary documents unless you are authorized to provide them.

Reference files and links

Provide photographs and specifications through reference links. A linked photograph or specification remains subject to the privacy and access settings of the service hosting it. Avoid links that publicly expose unrelated documents.

Cookies and analytics

Hosting and security providers may process necessary technical data to operate and protect the website. External source links have their own privacy practices.

Retention and access

Request information should be kept only while it is needed for review, an approved order, applicable recordkeeping or a legitimate security purpose. You may ask to access, correct or remove information you submitted through the online contact route, subject to applicable legal requirements and our ability to verify the request.

Cross-border processing and security

Providers and approved order partners may process information outside your country. Access should be limited to people and providers who need it for the request or order. No internet service can promise absolute security. Do not send sensitive documents through the general request form.

Questions or changes

Use the online request page for privacy enquiries. This page may be updated when our services or request-handling arrangements change. The date above identifies the current version.

Supplier verification and spam protection

Supplier forms use Cloudflare Turnstile where browser scripting is available. A verification token is checked by the request handler before a normal supplier submission is accepted. Applications submitted through the no-scripting review path are marked for separate manual verification before any introduction. A timer and a hidden spam-detection field also help protect request handling.

For the no-scripting review step, a short-lived verification record is kept for up to 30 minutes. It stores a digest of the form values and review timing rather than a public enquiry listing. Request information remains private and is used to review the enquiry and respond through the reply email.

Private enquiry notifications

Enquiry details can be sent to our internal Microsoft 365 mailbox so that we can review and respond to your request. Cloudflare processes the form and Microsoft processes the internal notification. Your reply address is used for the enquiry, not displayed publicly or added to a public supplier directory. If notification delivery needs another attempt, the saved request remains private while that attempt is made.
