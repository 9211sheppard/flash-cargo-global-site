Website and Request Terms

Updated 2026-10-24

These terms explain how Cargo Global’s website and request process work. The written quotation and order-specific agreement set the commercial terms for any purchase or delivery.

Requests are not orders

Submitting a request asks us to review requirements. It does not reserve stock, authorize a supplier purchase, create a delivery booking or authorize payment. An order proceeds only after the relevant quote and approval steps are agreed.

Product and supplier review

Availability, minimum quantities, sample options, production timing and supplier suitability depend on the product and request. You are responsible for providing accurate specifications and identifying any required certifications or acceptance criteria. We confirm the review scope with you; a supplier shortlist is not a guarantee of every product attribute.

Samples and inspections

Samples, factory information and independent inspection can support an approval decision. Sample charges, inspection scope, timing, acceptance criteria and any limitations must be set out in the quote or order agreement. No inspection can guarantee the absence of every defect or establish compliance beyond its agreed scope.

Price, currency and payment

Quotes identify USD or CAD and set out the agreed inclusions and exclusions. Product, samples, tooling, inspection, freight, customs, taxes, unloading and delivery are included only to the extent stated in the quote. Currency conversion, payment stages and any changes must be agreed before purchase. Do not send payment details through the website form.

Compliance and availability

We review requests subject to applicable import, export, product-safety and sanctions requirements. The phrase “Buy Anything” is an invitation to discuss a product, not an offer to source prohibited, counterfeit or otherwise unlawful goods. Products needing permits, testing, registration or special handling require review before acceptance.

Delivery and changes

Production and delivery schedules depend on supplier performance, inspection, transport, customs clearance and destination requirements. Estimated dates are not guaranteed unless the order agreement expressly says otherwise. Access restrictions, appointment requirements, unloading, changes and additional costs should be agreed in writing.

Cancellations, defects and claims

Cancellation, return, warranty, defect and transport-claim arrangements must be set out in the applicable supplier terms and order agreement before you approve the purchase. The website does not promise a general return or refund policy for every sourced product. Any mandatory legal rights continue to apply.

Guides and external sources

Guides are preparation aids, not a substitute for current advice from the responsible authority or a qualified adviser. Rules may change. External sources are independently operated and are not endorsements or evidence of a partnership with Cargo Global.

Website content and privacy

Do not misuse the site, upload unlawful material or submit information you have no right to share. Request handling is described in the Privacy Policy. Order-specific written terms and applicable law govern any transaction; this general page does not replace them.

Our payment policy

A written quote comes first

Nothing is paid before a written, itemized quote. The quote identifies the product, quantity, currency, delivery scope, inclusions, exclusions and the agreed payment stages.

Supplier payment is staged

Supplier payment is staged: a deposit, then the balance only after sample approval and any agreed pre-shipment inspection. The approved sample, agreed checks and acceptance decision belong in the order record.

Pay a business, not a person

Payments go only to a business account or a recognized processor’s invoice, never a personal account. The business payee and payment instructions must match the written order agreement.

Verify any change before paying

We never change bank or payment details by email. Any change is confirmed through the request form before you pay.

No surprise fee requests

No surprise “release”, “customs” or “insurance” fees outside the written quote. Any proposed change must be itemized and agreed in writing before payment.

Agree the remedy before payment

If goods fail inspection, the refund or replacement terms are written plainly. The order agreement identifies the remedy, responsibilities, applicable costs and timing before payment; no unspecified automatic refund, escrow or insurance protection is implied.

We only email from @flashcargoglobal.com and never request payment by gift card, crypto or a personal account.
